**Staff Mode** isolates staff members from ordinary gameplay, preventing accidental or deliberate item drops, damage, or world tampering, while granting rapid moderation tools.

Toggle Staff Mode using:

```bash
/staff
```

:::note[Required Permissions]
To enter Staff Mode, a player must possess both the base staff identifier permission `nookure.staff` and `nookure.staff.mode`.
:::

***

## Protective Mechanics & Restrictions

When a staff member enters Staff Mode, NookureStaff enforces strict gameplay boundaries:

1. **Inventory Preservation**:
   * The player's survival inventory, armor, and offhand items are safely saved into memory and cleared.
   * Configured staff items from `items.yml` are loaded into the player's hotbar.
   * When exiting Staff Mode, the original inventory is restored down to the exact NBT data and slot locations.
2. **Item & Inventory Lock**:
   * Dropping items (`PlayerDropItemEvent`) is cancelled.
   * Picking up items (`EntityPickupItemEvent`) is cancelled.
   * Swapping offhand items and moving items in inventories are cancelled.
3. **Damage & Entity Protection**:
   * Staff members cannot take damage from any source (PvP, fall, environmental).
   * Staff members cannot attack entities or players.
   * Hostile monsters ignore staff members and will not target them (`EntityTargetLivingEntityEvent`).
4. **Environment & Spawners**:
   * Monster spawners do not activate in the proximity of vanished staff members (Paper 1.21+).
   * Advancement criterion grants are blocked.
   * Food level changes (hunger loss) are cancelled.

***

## Staff Mode Utilities

### Silent Chest Viewing

Staff members can inspect chests, barrels, shulker boxes, and ender chests without triggering open/close animations or chest creaking sounds.

* Configurable in `config.yml`: `staffMode.silentChestOpen: true`.

### Previous Location Teleportation

When exiting Staff Mode, staff members can automatically be teleported back to the exact location they were at prior to enabling staff mode.

* Configurable in `config.yml`: `staffMode.teleportToPreviousLocation: true`.

### Night Vision & Custom Effects

* Staff mode automatically applies Night Vision so staff can inspect dark caves or unlit builds.
* Additional potion effects (e.g., Speed, Jump Boost) can be specified under `staffMode.customPotionEffects`.

### Action Bar HUD

Staff members receive real-time telemetry on their action bar showing:

* Vanish status (`{vanished}`)
* Current chat destination channel (`{staffChat}`)
* Real-time server TPS (`{tps}`)
* Toggle permission: `nookure.staff.actionbar`

## Blocked Commands in Staff Mode

To prevent abuse or accidental command executions (e.g. running economy or gameplay commands while in staff mode), commands can be restricted in `staff-mode-blocked-commands.yml`:

```yaml title="staff-mode-blocked-commands.yml"
blockedCommands:
  - "pay"
  - "trade"
  - "ah"
  - "auction"
  - "tpa"
  - "tpahere"

message: "<red>You cannot execute this command while in Staff Mode!</red>"
```

### Bypass Permission

Staff administrators can bypass blocked commands by holding the permission:

```text
nookure.staff.mode.commands.bypass
```
