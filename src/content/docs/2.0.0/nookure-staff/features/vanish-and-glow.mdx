NookureStaff includes built-in invisibility and visual identification systems designed to keep staff members invisible to regular players while clearly distinguishable to other team members.

***

## Vanish System (`/vanish` or `/v`)

Toggling vanish hides the staff member from:

* Ordinary players in the world.
* Server tab lists and player counts (`%nkstaff_server_count%`).
* Entity targeting and ambient mob reactions.

### Vanish Providers

In `config.yml`, configure `staffMode.vanishType`:

* **`INTERNAL_VANISH`** *(Default)*: Uses NookureStaff's native packet-level hiding engine without external dependencies.
* **`SUPER_VANISH`**: Seamlessly delegates vanish management to the SuperVanish plugin.
* **`PREMIUM_VANISH`**: Seamlessly delegates vanish management to the PremiumVanish plugin.

### Fake Join / Quit Broadcasts

To prevent players from deducing when staff enter or exit vanish, NookureStaff can broadcast fake quit and join messages:

* When enabling vanish: broadcasts a fake quit message to online non-staff players.
* When disabling vanish: broadcasts a fake join message.
* Configurable in `messages.yml`.

### Seeing Vanished Players

Staff members holding the permission:

```text
nookure.staff.vanish.see
```

can see other vanished staff members as translucent characters and see them in the `/staff list` GUI.

***

## Glow & Nametag System (`glow.yml`)

The Glow system applies a colored glowing outline to staff members so other staff can quickly spot teammates across distances.

```yaml title="plugins/NookureStaff/glow.yml"
# Enable or disable the glow feature
enabled: true

# Change the prefix color of the player's name
tab-integration: true

# The default color for the glow
default-color: <white>

# This colors must follow the format: <color> or <#hex>
glow-colors:
  admin: <yellow>
  staff: <red>
  mod: <green>
```

### TAB Integration

When `tabIntegration: true` is set, NookureStaff hooks into NEZNAMY's **TAB** plugin to automatically match overhead nametag prefixes and tablist team colors to the staff member's glow color.
