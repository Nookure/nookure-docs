The Freeze module lets staff members halt a suspect player during an investigation (such as screensharing, hack checks, or rule infractions).

***

## Freezing a Player (`/freeze`)

```bash
/freeze <player>
```

When a player is frozen:

1. **Movement & Physics Cancelled**: Movement, jumping, falling, and teleportation are locked.
2. **Interactions Blocked**: Breaking blocks, placing blocks, opening chests, dropping items, and attacking entities are disabled.
3. **Chat Banner**: A repeating chat title/message informs the player they are under investigation, instructs them to join your Discord or voice channel, and shows a remaining countdown timer.

:::note[Bypass Immunity]
Players holding `nookure.staff.freeze.bypass` cannot be frozen.
:::

***

## Countdown Timer & Pause Functionality

In `config.yml`, configure `freeze.freezeTimer` (default 300 seconds / 5 minutes).

* The frozen player has until the timer hits 0 to cooperate.
* If the countdown expires before the player is unfrozen, NookureStaff automatically executes configured commands (e.g., `/ban {player} 30d Refusing Screenshare`).

### Pausing the Countdown

If the suspect is cooperating (e.g. joining Discord or sharing screens) and needs extra time, staff can pause the timer:

```bash
/freeze <player> pause
```

Running the command again unpauses the countdown.

***

## Combatting Combat Loggers / Quitting Under Freeze

If a frozen player disconnects while under investigation, NookureStaff provides interactive exit handling via `config.yml`:

* **Interactive Staff Notification**:
  When the player leaves, staff members receive an interactive clickable prompt in chat:
  ```text
  [!] Player Steve disconnected while frozen!
  [Click to Punish: ✔]   [Click to Dismiss: ✖]
  ```
* **Executing the Punishment**:
  * Clicking `[✔]` executes `/freeze /exec <uuid>`, automatically issuing the configured evasion punishment (e.g. 30-day ban).
  * Clicking `[✖]` executes `/freeze /remove <uuid>`, dismissing the pending sanction without punishing the player.

***

## Isolated Freeze Chat (`/freezechat`)

To communicate privately with the suspect without public chat distractions or letting other players read the screenshare instructions:

```bash
/freezechat <player> <message>
```

Only the target player and staff members with the freeze permission can see messages sent through this channel.
