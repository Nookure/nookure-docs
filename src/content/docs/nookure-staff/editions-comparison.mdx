## What's Included in NookureStaff+

### 1. Web Viewer (Beta)
Connects your Minecraft servers to the **Akko** real-time WebSocket web dashboard:
- **Live Server Telemetry**: Streams TPS, MSPT, RAM usage, CPU load, and online staff/player states.
- **Remote Moderation & Auditing**: Query action logs and punishment history directly from the browser.
- **Remote Sanctions & Evidence**: Issue punishments, attach evidence, and receive real-time sanction broadcasts over WebSockets.
- **Permission-Aware Multicast & Auth**: `/akko auth <key>` session authentication with permission-grouped packet broadcasting.

### 2. Punishments & Sanctions Suite
A complete, network-ready moderation framework:
- **Full Sanction Types**: Permanent and temporary Bans, IP-Bans, Mutes, IP-Mutes, Warnings, and Kicks with silent (`-s`) support and Velocity proxy pre-login enforcement.
- **Interactive `/punish` GUI**: Visual player selector, sanction type menu, material-icon templates, and confirmation dialogs to prevent accidental sanctions.
- **Warning Auto-Escalation**: Categorized warnings with decay timers, automatic escalation ladders, and smart sanction rollback on `/unwarn`.
- **Mandatory Evidence Policy & S3 Uploads**: Rank-based evidence deadlines with auto-pardon enforcement, `/evidence` management, and presigned URL uploads for AWS S3, MinIO, and Cloudflare R2.
- **Alt Account Management (`/dupeip`)**: Automatic IP-to-account linking, clickable join alerts (`[Approve]` / `[Ban]`), and staff approval workflows.
- **Granular Pardon & Protection**: Separate permissions for pardoning or removing evidence created by other staff (`nookure.staff.punishments.pardon.others`, `nookure.staff.evidence.remove.others`), plus self/staff IP-punish protection.

### 3. Inventory Rollback
Automated snapshot and recovery system to protect players from bugs, rollbacks, or unfair deaths:
- **Automatic Triggers**: Captures GZIP-compressed snapshots on `DEATH`, `JOIN`, `QUIT`, `WORLD_CHANGE`, `/clear`, and staff inventory edits (`STAFF_MODIFIED`).
- **Categories & Preview GUI**: Browse snapshots by trigger category, inspect items/stats, and duplicate items directly (`/rollback preview`).
- **6 Restoration Modes**: Restore `ALL`, `INVENTORY_ONLY`, `ARMOR_ONLY`, `ENDER_CHEST_ONLY`, `EXPERIENCE_ONLY`, or `DROP_AT_LOCATION` (with per-server filtering).
- **Automatic Background Purging**: Configurable retention days and periodic `InventorySnapshotPurgeTask` cleanup.

### 4. Actions (Action Logger)
Forensic database auditing for complete staff and player accountability:
- **Granular Tracking**: Configurable logging for staff mode toggles, vanish changes, freezes, inventory inspections/modifications, rollbacks, note edits, alt lookups/approvals, and player chat/commands/logins.
- **Async Virtual-Thread Persistence**: Stores actor, target, server name, coordinates, timestamp, and structured JSON metadata without blocking the main server thread.
- **Evidence Tagging & Search**: Supports name search, pagination filters, and `--actions` linking from `/evidence add`.

### 5. Infrastructure & Quality-of-Life Enhancements
- **Distributed & Local Cache (`cache.yml`)**: Pluggable **Caffeine** (in-memory) and **Redis** (distributed) caching layer for active punishments, player metadata, and pre-login checks.
- **PlaceholderAPI in Staff Chat & Broadcasts**: Full PlaceholderAPI parsing inside staff chat messages and sanction broadcasts.
- **GlitchTip Error Reporting**: Opt-out automatic exception reporting for faster bug detection and resolution.
